Old Fashioned Apple Crisp

Below are a list of ingredients you will need for this recipe:

Spiced Apples

  • At least 6 golden delicious apples peeled and chopped
  • 2 Tbsp Granulated sugar
  • 3/4 tsp Ground Cinnamon
  • 1 1/2 tsp Lemon juice

Crisp Topping

  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 3/4 cup old fashioned oats
  • 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • pinch of kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter diced into small cubes

Now that you have all of the ingredients to make this recipe, follow the instructions below:

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350 F degrees. Butter an 8x8 baking dish, or spray with non-stick cooking spray. Set aside
  2. To make the spiced apples, in a mixing bowl, add the chopped apples, 2 Tbsp granulated sugar, 3/4 tsp of the cinnamon and 1 1/2 tsp lemon juice. Stir to combine, then transfer to prepared baking dish
  3. To make the topping, first, in a seperate mixing bowl, add the topping ingredients of 1 cup brown sugar, 3/4 cup oats, 3/4 cup all-purpose flour, 1 tsp cinnamon, pinch of kosher salt, and the 1/2 cup of diced cold butter.
  4. Continuing with the topping, use a pastry cutter to cut the butter into the oat mixture, using a slight downward twisting motion, until the mixture resembles pea-sized crumbs. If you do not have a pastry cutter, you can use two forks or your hands to cut butter into the mixture.
  5. Now onto baking. Spread the topping over the apples in a baking dish, and gently pat to even it out. Bake 40-50 minutes, until golden brown and bubbly.
  6. Now that your Old-fashioned Apple Crisp is all done, serve warm and enjoy. We highly recommend serving some vanilla ice cream and/or whip cream!
